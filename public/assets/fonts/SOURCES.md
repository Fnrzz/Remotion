# Offline Plus Jakarta Sans fonts

These unmodified WOFF2 files are the exact Google Fonts **v12 normal variable**
assets previously loaded by
`node_modules/@remotion/google-fonts/dist/cjs/PlusJakartaSans.js` (`getInfo()`).
They retain the **Plus Jakarta Sans** family, **200–800** weight axis, and original
`latin` and `latin-ext` Unicode ranges. `src/fonts.ts` loads only these local files
through Remotion's `staticFile()`; rendering needs no Google Fonts connection.
