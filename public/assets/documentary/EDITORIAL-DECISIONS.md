## Why no article screenshot / official artwork is embedded

- https://blog.google/press/ says **images on that page** may be published with
  credit “Source: Google.” This is not a blanket license for all blog imagery.
- https://about.google/brand-resource-center/guidance/media/ distinguishes brand
  guidance from copyright permission and says fair-use analysis is fact-specific.
- No explicit license covering the Carbon article screenshot, header artwork,
  or benchmark figures was established. None was downloaded or used as a visual.
- Instead, scenes 02 and 05 use **original, concise factual editorial summaries**
  attributed to the genuine source URL. They are labeled RINGKASAN REDAKSI, not
  DOKUMENTASI RESMI. They are not quotations, Google-designed pages, screenshots,
  or reconstructed Google interfaces.
- An authentic licensed 2017 office archive photo provides contextual imagery,
  clearly marked as not documenting the Carbon team.
- The existing unchanged Gemini wordmark is retained in the closing. Its earlier
  PD-textlogo / trademark provenance remains in `../branding/` (repository path:
  `public/assets/branding/google-gemini-SOURCES.md`). The separate editorial
  “4 CARBON” label is not represented as an official Carbon-specific logo.
