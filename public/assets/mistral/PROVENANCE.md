## Model licensing — important qualifications

The controlling text [D] is the **Mistral Ecosystem Open-Weight License**, explicitly a **modified MIT license**, not the unmodified MIT license or Apache 2.0. Prefer “open-weight dengan syarat lisensi” over an unqualified “open source bebas untuk semua.”

1. **General permission:** use, copy, modify, publish, distribute, sublicense, sell, deploy, further train, and create derivatives are permitted, subject to the conditions. The defined “Model” includes weights, parameters, configuration files, and **associated documentation**. Section 1 requires the copyright and permission notices in all copies or substantial portions of that material. The attribution notice is `2026 - Mistral`.
2. **MaaS businesses and affiliates:** section 2 applies if **the licensee OR ANY OF ITS AFFILIATES operates a Model-as-a-Service business**. In that case, the licensee must obtain a separate Mistral commercial license before using the model **or derivatives for any commercial purpose**, subject to section 3. It is not limited to selling this particular model as an API; the affiliate/business trigger is broader than that simplification.
3. **Definition matters:** MaaS means third-party access to the model/derivatives for inference or post-training, by any means, with meaningful control over inputs, parameters, or training data. The definition expressly excludes (a) end-user products whose model capabilities are solely embedded in specific features or harnesses and (b) merely relaying requests to models hosted by others. Those exclusions do not erase a separate affiliate's qualifying MaaS business.
4. **Internal-use exception:** section 3(a) says section 2 does **not** apply to internal use, defined as use that does not make the model or derivatives available to third parties. Thus, “every MaaS-affiliated company always needs a commercial license even for internal use” is inaccurate.
5. **Official-product exception:** section 3(b) also excludes from section 2 any use of the model accessed through Mistral's official products. Product/API terms, pricing, and other applicable obligations remain separate; this is not a promise of a free service.
6. **No warranty:** the license supplies the model and outputs “AS IS,” disclaims warranties including noninfringement, and limits Mistral's liability. It does not clear third-party rights in inputs, outputs, or independent source material.

Concise Indonesian explainer: **“Lisensinya mengizinkan banyak penggunaan, termasuk komersial. Namun, bisnis MaaS—termasuk bila afiliasinya menjalankan MaaS—perlu lisensi komersial terpisah untuk penggunaan komersial, dengan pengecualian penggunaan internal dan akses lewat produk resmi Mistral.”** Link the full license; this sentence is not a substitute for the definitions above.
