## Claim decisions

- **November 12, 2026 launch: supported.** Both Google announcements and the model card carry that date; AA independently lists the same Hydrogen release date. Say “diumumkan 12 November 2026,” not an unqualified guarantee of worldwide account availability.
- **High thinking 51 vs Argon 50: independently corroborated.** Both AA model pages show those scores on Intelligence Index v5.0.1. Google says High is Hydrogen’s default. These are index points, not percentages or proof of superiority on every task; the gap is one point.
- **Orchestrator combines Carbon and Hydrogen: supported as a Google product claim.** Antigravity describes bidirectional switching within a session, not merely static first-prompt routing. Do not conflate this with a claim that every session always launches parallel agents.
- **24% less quota / 19% faster: vendor-reported only.** Both Google launch and Antigravity pages give those numbers, describing near-Carbon task success. Quota is not the same as API dollar cost or token count; “near” is not identical performance. These are average evaluation results, not universal user guarantees.
- **Terminal-Bench version: unresolved source conflict.** The main launch article attaches the Orchestrator figures to **4.0**, while the dedicated Antigravity announcement attaches the same figures to **4.1**. Do not silently choose one or label them interchangeable. Safest narration omits the version and attributes the claim to Google; show the discrepancy in source notes.
- **API $1.50 / $7.50: supported by current official pricing and AA.** Specify **USD per million input/output tokens, Standard paid tier**, including thinking in output billing. Batch/Flex and Priority have different rates; grounding/tool charges may be additional. It is not a subscription price or a flat fee per request.

## Explicit verification limits

**Independent verification blocked for the Orchestrator 24%/19% measurements and their exact benchmark version.** The inspected independent Terminal-Bench page does not provide those measurements; no run-level reproduction was performed.
